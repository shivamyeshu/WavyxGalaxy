# Complete Tutorial Guide

## Publishing Workflows

### How to Publish a Workflow

Publishing allows you to share your workflows with anyone via a public link. Here's how:

1. **Create Your Workflow**
   - Add nodes (Text Input, Image, Video, LLM, etc.)
   - Connect them with edges
   - Configure each node with your data

2. **Save Your Workflow**
   - Click the **SAVE** button in the top-right corner
   - Your workflow will auto-save every 3 seconds after changes

3. **Publish Your Workflow**
   - Click the **PUBLISH** button (megaphone icon) in the header
   - Sign in if you haven't already

4. **Publishing Options**
   
   **First Time Publishing:**
   - A success modal will appear with your shareable link
   - Link is automatically copied to clipboard
   - You can manually copy it again from the modal
   
   **Already Published?**
   - You'll see two options:
     - **Update Existing** - Keeps the same link, updates the workflow content
     - **Publish as New** - Creates a completely new shareable link

5. **Share Your Link**
   - Copy the link from the modal
   - Share it via email, social media, or messaging apps
   - Anyone with the link can view your workflow (no login required)

### Managing Published Workflows

1. **View Your Published Workflows**
   - Click **My publishes** in the left sidebar
   - See all your published workflows with links and timestamps

2. **Copy a Published Link**
   - Click the **Copy** button next to any published workflow
   - Share the link anywhere

3. **Delete a Published Workflow**
   - Click the **Delete** button (trash icon)
   - Confirm deletion
   - The link will stop working immediately

## Accessing Published Workflows

### How to View a Shared Workflow

1. **Open the Share Link**
   - Click on a shared workflow link
   - No login required to view

2. **Explore the Workflow**
   - See all nodes and their connections
   - View workflow configuration
   - Pan and zoom to navigate
   - Use zoom controls (bottom-left)
   - Use minimap (bottom-right)

3. **Use the Workflow**
   - Click **Use this workflow** button
   - You'll be prompted to sign in
   - A copy is created in your workspace
   - Edit and customize it as your own

4. **Copy the Link**
   - Click **Copy Link** to share with others
   - Link remains active even after copying

### Features of Published Workflows

✅ **Read-Only View** - Viewers can see but not edit the original
✅ **Public Access** - No login required to view
✅ **Duplicate Capability** - Signed-in users can create their own copy
✅ **Permanent Links** - Links remain active until you delete them
✅ **Multiple Versions** - Publish as new to create version 2, 3, etc.
✅ **Update in Place** - Update existing to keep the same link

## Tips & Best Practices

### Publishing Tips
- Give your workflow a clear, descriptive name before publishing
- Test your workflow with the **RUN WORKFLOW** button before publishing
- Use **Update Existing** for bug fixes and minor changes
- Use **Publish as New** for major revisions or different versions

### Sharing Tips
- Add context when sharing links (what the workflow does)
- Check "My publishes" regularly to manage your shared workflows
- Delete old/unused published workflows to keep things organized

### Privacy Tips
- Anyone with the link can view your workflow
- Published workflows are publicly accessible
- Don't include sensitive data in published workflows
- You can delete a published workflow anytime to revoke access
